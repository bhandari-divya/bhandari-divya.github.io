Introduction

About

I am a PhD candidate in Population Health Sciences at the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health, working at the intersection of epidemiology, decision science, and global health.

My research uses population-based modeling and simulation to estimate potential long-term and intergenerational health benefits of preventive interventions. I also have research experience in cancer prevention, migrant health, and population health in underserved settings.

Before Harvard, I earned a Master of Health Science from the University of Tokyo and a Bachelor of Science in Nursing from Tribhuvan University.
