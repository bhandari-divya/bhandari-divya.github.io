Research

Research that connects early investments to later health

My research centers on prevention across the life course, with an emphasis on quantitative approaches that link epidemiologic evidence to population-level decisions.

Research agenda

Long-term & intergenerational health

Many preventive interventions are assessed primarily for their immediate benefits. I investigate their potential to influence chronic disease risks decades later and across generations.

My work integrates epidemiologic effect estimates, demographic projections, and population health data through mathematical modeling and life-course simulation. The goal is to estimate possible health outcomes under alternative intervention coverage scenarios—not to suggest that modeled impacts have already occurred.
